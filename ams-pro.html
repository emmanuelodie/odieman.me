AMS Pro

Turn the exports your hospital system already produces — pharmacy dispensing, admissions, laboratory and culture results — into a working antimicrobial stewardship programme: AWaRe and consumption analysis, an antibiogram, statistically sound trend charts, patient-level review lists, CRFs, and reports ready for the AMS committee.

Open the live demo Request AMS Pro

What it takes in, what it gives back

Input

A pharmacy dispensing export (item name, quantity, date — plus dosage, ward, price and prescriber where available), optionally with the admissions register, laboratory results with turnaround times, culture & sensitivity results and a procedures report. Several files per type are merged automatically, so three yearly exports become one three-year analysis.

Output

AWaRe shares under WHO and Tanzania rules, DDD and DOT per 1,000 patient-days, monthly trends, run charts, an antibiogram, daily review lists, cost analysis, indicators and CRFs — and a Deep Report as an editable Word document and PowerPoint deck.

The modules

25 modules in four groups, each explained inside the tool's built-in user guide.

Clinical records

Patients · Prescriptions · Antibiogram — one line per patient joined across pharmacy, lab, admissions and procedures; every dispensing line with its matched generic, AWaRe class, route, days and DDD; and a cumulative antibiogram counting the first isolate per patient per organism (CLSI M39), with cells under 30 isolates flagged.

AMS operations

Dashboard · Antibiotic Time-out · Bug–drug Check · Consumption · Cost & Impact · Analytics · Run Charts · Driver Zones · Resistance Trend · What's Working · Discrepancy · PDCA — the daily review list (IV ≥3 days, double anaerobic cover, long courses, Reserve without culture, prophylaxis beyond 24 h), mismatches between culture results and the antibiotic given, Laney p′ control charts, trend lines with confidence bands, spend by AWaRe tier, and recognition for wards and prescribers doing well.

Reporting & indicators

Reports · Indicators · WHO/INRUD · CRF Forms · Export — the Deep Report in Word and PowerPoint, Tanzania GHS-CQI AMS and IPC indicators, WHO/INRUD prescribing indicators, CRF01 dispensing, CRF02 SSI after caesarean section and CRF03 audit log.

Setup & data

User Guide · Setup · Dose Reference · Data Entry · Import Hub — facility details, the AWaRe scheme, an editable WHO ATC/DDD dose reference, manual entry, and an import hub that recognises messy real-world exports (title rows, repeated headers, missing columns) and can undo any single import.

Saving

Data folder — choose a folder once and everything is saved there automatically: the database, a daily backup, a safety copy before any reset or restore, and every export and report. Nothing in the folder is overwritten without being read first.
