Run to the Name

The Names of God for Every Storm of Life

When the storm comes, where do you run? Nineteen names of God, from Elohim, who spoke light into the darkness, to Abba, who ran down the road to meet a wasted son. Each chapter sets one name beside one storm of modern life, most of them in Tanzania: a flood in Jangwani, a bribe in Dodoma, a family court under a mango tree, a delivery room in the dark. And every chapter ends at the same door: Jesus Christ.

Emmanuel Odie The Watermelon Moon A Chronicle of the Rind

The Watermelon Moon

A Chronicle of the Rind

The Moon is not a rock. It is the heart of a colossal watermelon, and inside it the ancient world is still alive: Babylon never fell, Rome never ended, and Archimedes, Pythagoras, Kepler and Caesar measure, rhyme and quarrel about calendars. Then, from outside, comes a knock. The story is told on a veranda in Kyela, by Lake Nyasa, by a retired mathematics teacher to two grandchildren who will not stop interrupting. The world is invented. The mathematics and the science are real.

A Prologue, thirteen Moons and an Epilogue · 208-page PDF · a Who’s Who of the real and the invented
