What the full version gives you

  • The three tools as single files that open in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Nothing to install, and no internet needed.
  • They read the exports your hospital system already produces (pharmacy dispensing, admissions, laboratory) and your PPS forms.
  • Your work saves automatically to a data folder on your own computer, and nothing leaves it unless you choose to share it.
  • A built-in user guide and sample data in every tool, so new staff can learn before touching real data.
  • Your copy is registered to your facility, so news of updates and fixes reaches you.
