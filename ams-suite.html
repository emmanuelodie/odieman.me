AMS Suite

Turn a raw pharmacy dispensing export into a full antimicrobial stewardship programme: consumption analysis, cost and savings modeling, IPC linkage, and a quarterly action-tracking cycle — all from one file, run offline.

Download AMS Suite See the methodology

What it takes in, what it gives back

Input

A pharmacy dispensing export (CSV/Excel) — item name, quantity, department, date, price. The kind of file most hospital pharmacy systems can already produce, however messy the formatting.

Output

Ward and prescriber-level dashboards, an antibiogram, a cost and savings model, a quarterly AMS committee report ready to export, and a running PDCA action list with named owners and deadlines.

The modules

36 modules, organized here by what they're for — not an exhaustive list of every screen, but the shape of the tool.

Consumption & classification

Prescribing · Prescriptions · Antibiogram · AWaRe · AMR Drivers — DDD/100 patient-days, AWaRe (Access/Watch/Reserve) classification with Tanzania STG overrides, and drug-resistance driver tracking, all drillable to ward and prescriber level.

Cost & economics

Economics · Hospital Cost · AMS Budget · What-if · EMD What-if — patient-borne vs. insurance-borne cost breakdowns, a transparent linear cost trend (never called a forecast), and adjustable savings scenarios for OPD/EMD prescribing changes.

Quality improvement

PDCA · Findings · Wins · Tasks · Compliance · Performance — a running Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle: findings get logged, assigned an owner and deadline, tracked to completion, and wins get recorded so the committee has a real audit trail quarter to quarter.

Infection prevention & control

IPC · IPC-AS · Rounds · Lab — hand hygiene, PPE, sharps safety and housekeeping indicators scored against Tanzania's National IPC Guidelines 2018, linked directly to the antimicrobial data so a surgical site infection rate and a prophylaxis-duration finding sit side by side.

Programme operations

Meetings · Team · KAPs · Patients · Departments · Trends · Analytics · Aggregator — committee meeting minutes, a knowledge-attitudes-practice survey module, department-level rollups, and multi-month trend aggregation across everything above.

Output & reference

Reports · Export · Manual · Glossary · Feedback · Settings — Word/PDF report generation, a built-in manual and glossary (bilingual English/Swahili), and facility settings for beds, occupancy, and reporting period.
